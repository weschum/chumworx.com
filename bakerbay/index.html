Baker Bay DGC · Round 2 Cards

Lowest score wins. Ties share a place (T2). Event total shows once both rounds are entered.

Round 1 groups

Paste updated Round 1 hole assignments in the same format you send them (Hole 1 then 1. MPO: Name lines) and tap Update. Scores already entered stay attached to each player by name.

Round 2 hole rules

Within each pool, players are ranked by Round 1 total (lowest first) and handed out top-down in this order. Hole 18 keeps its Round 1 group and those players are left out of the pools. On every card the lowest Round 1 score throws first.

PDGA results

For the TD Report upload in Tournament Manager. Copy, then paste into Upload with “First row in results contain field names” ticked, and set T.O. and Place to blank on Field Mappings. A DNF gets 999 in the round they didn't finish; a no-show is left off.
