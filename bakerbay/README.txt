BAKER BAY DGC – ROUND 2 APP (offline home-screen app)

UPLOAD (one time)
1. Upload this whole "bakerbay" folder to your chumworx.com server so the files sit at:
     https://chumworx.com/bakerbay/index.html   (plus the other files next to it)
   It must be HTTPS - offline mode does not work over plain http.
2. Open https://chumworx.com/bakerbay/ on a computer to confirm it loads.

INSTALL ON IPHONE (while you have signal)
1. Open https://chumworx.com/bakerbay/ in SAFARI (not Chrome).
2. Tap Share -> "Add to Home Screen" -> Add.
3. Open the new "Baker Bay" icon once while still online. That saves it to the phone.
4. Test: turn on Airplane Mode, open the icon again. It should open and work.

ON EVENT DAY
- Always open it from the home-screen icon. The icon and the Safari tab keep
  separate data, so scores typed in one will not show in the other.
- Before closing out Round 1, tap Setup -> "Export scores (CSV)" as a backup.

UPDATING LATER
- Replace index.html on the server, then open sw.js and change
  VERSION = "bakerbay-v1" to "bakerbay-v2" (and so on) and upload it too.
- Open the home-screen app once with signal; close and reopen it to get the new version.
  Scores already entered stay on the phone.
